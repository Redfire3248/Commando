  • Pick one of five agents. Each has their own health, speed and ability; everyone carries the same rifle.
  • You have hearts: a bullet takes one, a bomb two. Water is instant death.
  • The team shares one pool of lives. When it runs out, a fallen player waits for the next stage.
  • First-aid kits heal 2 hearts, the big medkit heals the whole team, the medal gives the team a life.
  • Add bots to fill your team. Up to 5 players on one screen, or play online with friends.
  • Hold a direction while shooting to aim 8 ways. Down on the ground lies flat. Down + Jump drops through a ledge.
  • Esc or P pauses. F9 opens the admin panel (scores are not saved after using it).
