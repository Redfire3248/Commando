WHO IS PLAYING?

Everyone press your FIRE button to join

Keyboard 1: F (moves with W A S D, G jumps) · Keyboard 2: K (moves with arrows, L jumps) · Gamepad: A or X. Playing alone on a keyboard? You get both layouts.

  • Up to 5 players on one screen: two on the keyboard, the rest on gamepads (or one on a touch screen).
  • Hold a direction while shooting to aim 8 ways. Hold up to shoot straight up.
  • Hold down on the ground to lie flat — most shots pass over you.
  • Down + Jump on a ledge drops through it.
  • Shoot the flying capsules for power-ups: R rapid fire, S shield, 1 extra life.
  • Anything that touches you kills you. Falling in the water kills you.
  • Esc or P pauses.
