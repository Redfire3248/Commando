  • Pick an agent. Each has their own health, speed and ability; everyone carries the same rifle.
  • Bullets take a heart, bombs two, water is instant death. The squad shares one pool of lives.
  • Hide behind cover: crates, sandbags and barriers stop every shot — yours too. You can stand on them.
  • First-aid kits heal 2 hearts, the big medkit heals everyone, the medal is +1 squad life.
  • Hold a direction while shooting to aim 8 ways. Down on the ground lies flat. Down + Jump drops off a ledge.
  • On a phone: thumb anywhere on the left half to move, FIRE / JUMP / SKILL on the right (Settings has a D-pad and auto fire).
